Privacy Policy

Watch Room is not affiliated with or endorsed by Twitch.

Data we collect

  • Your Twitch login (user id) when you log in.
  • The channels you choose to publish as "currently watching".
  • Subscription relationships you create with other users.
  • Publisher settings (subscription mode, visibility).

How we use it

To provide the watch-state sharing and feed features. We do not sell your data.

Retention & deletion

Data is kept while your account is active. Logging out deletes your watch state, settings, and subscriptions from our servers.

Third parties

We use Twitch's API to resolve channel metadata. No other third parties receive your data.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact the developer.
